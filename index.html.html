A Letter From My Heart

Dear you,

I am genuinely sorry. Not the kind of sorry that is only said because someone is upset, but the kind that comes from realizing that my actions affected someone who means so much to me.

I know I cannot go back and change what happened. I cannot erase a moment just by wishing it never happened. But I can tell you honestly how I feel, accept where I was wrong, and try to become better because you are worth that effort.

You are incredibly important to me. Sometimes I may fail to show it perfectly, but your place in my heart has not disappeared. I haven't suddenly become a different person who stopped caring. I am still me — the same person who cares about your smile, your feelings, your happiness and your presence.

And my love for you isn't getting smaller with time. It keeps growing. Every day I discover another reason to appreciate you, another memory to treasure, and another reason to be grateful that you are part of my life.

I'm sorry for the moments when my actions made you question any of that. You deserved reassurance, care and understanding, and I want to do better.

With all my heart, always. ❤️
